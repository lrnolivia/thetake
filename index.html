• Base pay is whichever is greater — your commission rate on revenue, or the minimum-wage floor for the hours worked. All rates live in Settings and are saved in this browser only.

    
• FICA (Social Security + Medicare) applies to all pay including tips and floor wages. Federal income tax is an estimated annual liability spread across the year, not a literal per-paycheck withholding figure — your actual withholding depends on your W-4 / 1099 status.

    
• Screenshot scanning uses on-device OCR (Tesseract.js) — the image never leaves your browser. It's best-effort: always check the parsed numbers before trusting them, and correct any field by hand if it misreads.

    
• Minimum wage and tax bracket figures in Settings are reference defaults, not live data — a static site like this one can't reliably auto-fetch government rate tables. Use the lookup links in Settings to confirm current numbers for your state.

    
• This is a planning and reconciliation tool, not tax advice — actual pay and tax owed will vary with real bookings, service mix, tips, and your filing situation.
